# Lob — Landing Page v0

> **Status:** Brand / product concept draft  
> **Purpose:** Source material for a simple initial landing page  
> **Initial vertical:** Tennis professionals and instructors  
> **Long-term direction:** Conversational operations assistant for independent professionals

---

## 1. Core Idea

Independent professionals already run a large part of their business through WhatsApp.

They do not necessarily need another system to feed, another agenda to maintain, or another workflow to learn.

Their operation is already happening — but it is trapped inside conversations.

Clients ask for times. Appointments are confirmed. Lessons are cancelled. Replacements are negotiated. Payments are discussed. Receipts are sent. Pending issues accumulate.

**Lob turns those conversations into an organized operation.**

The professional keeps talking to clients normally. Lob works in the background, helping transform conversational activity into structured information such as:

- appointments;
- cancellations and rescheduling;
- clients;
- payments;
- pending actions;
- reminders;
- operational summaries.

The central idea is not to replace WhatsApp.

**It is to make WhatsApp operationally useful.**

---

## 2. The Product Thesis

### Your work already happens on WhatsApp. Why leave it?

Most productivity software asks the user to change behavior:

1. receive a message from a client;
2. open another application;
3. create or update an appointment;
4. register a payment;
5. add a reminder;
6. periodically reconcile the system with what actually happened in conversations.

For a busy independent professional, this creates a second job: **administering the work after doing the work.**

Lob starts from the opposite premise:

> **Keep working the way you already work. We organize the operation around you.**

The conversation is the natural input layer.

The platform transforms that unstructured conversational flow into structured operational context.

---

## 3. The Problem

For many independent professionals, WhatsApp effectively acts as:

- inbox;
- CRM;
- scheduling interface;
- customer support channel;
- payment follow-up tool;
- reminder system;
- historical record.

But WhatsApp is fundamentally a conversation tool, not an operations system.

As the number of clients grows, information becomes fragmented across chats and days of conversation.

Typical problems include:

- forgetting an appointment agreed in a conversation;
- missing a cancellation or rescheduling request;
- losing track of replacement lessons;
- not remembering who has or has not paid;
- manually reconstructing the week's schedule;
- repeatedly searching old conversations for context;
- spending evenings organizing information generated during the day.

The problem is therefore not a lack of information.

**The information already exists. It is simply not organized.**

---

## 4. The Promise

Lob should make the professional feel that someone is quietly keeping the operation under control.

The desired outcomes are:

- more time;
- less administrative work;
- greater control;
- fewer forgotten commitments;
- more predictable routine;
- clearer financial visibility;
- lower cognitive load;
- peace of mind.

The product should feel less like "business software" and more like **having an assistant who understands what is happening.**

---

## 5. Why "Lob"?

The initial product experience is designed around tennis professionals.

In tennis, a lob is a high, deep shot often used when the opponent is pressuring the player near the net.

A good defensive lob creates distance and, crucially, **buys time for the player to recover position and regain control of the point.**

That creates a natural metaphor for the product.

The professional is under pressure:

- messages arriving;
- clients asking for times;
- cancellations;
- rescheduling;
- payments;
- a constantly changing agenda.

**Lob gives them time to recover.**

It does not play the game for them. It creates the space they need to stay in control.

### Core tennis line

> **Está sob pressão? O Lob resolve.**

### Concept behind the name

> When the point gets messy, a lob buys you time to recover.
>
> When your operation gets messy, Lob does the same.

This metaphor should influence the brand language without turning the product into a tennis joke. The identity can be playful, but the underlying benefit is serious.

## 6. Brand Architecture

Lob can initially be the tennis-facing identity of a broader technology platform.

This creates room for a vertical strategy:

```text
Core Platform
│
├── Lob — Tennis
│
├── Future vertical identity — Personal trainers
│
├── Future vertical identity — Beauty / salons
│
└── Future vertical identity — Other independent professionals
```

The technology and operational model can remain common while language, visual identity, onboarding, examples and workflows adapt to each profession.

This allows the business to **niche the go-to-market without permanently niching the product.**

---

## 7. Positioning

Lob is **not primarily an agenda application**.

An agenda is one structured output of the system.

The larger proposition is:

> **A conversational operations assistant for professionals whose business already happens on WhatsApp.**

This distinction matters because the long-term product can expand naturally from scheduling into:

- customer management;
- financial control;
- collections;
- reminders;
- recurring activities;
- operational analytics;
- business summaries;
- automated follow-ups;
- other administrative workflows.

The product starts with the agenda because scheduling is immediate, frequent and painful — not because scheduling defines the product.

## 9. Landing Page Narrative — v0

For the first landing page, simplicity is preferable to explaining the entire future platform.

The visitor should understand three things quickly:

1. **I can keep using WhatsApp.**
2. **Lob understands what is happening in my conversations and organizes it.**
3. **This saves me administrative work and gives me back time.**

### Suggested Hero

**Eyebrow**

`Seu assistente de quadra.`

**Headline**

> # Está sob pressão? O Lob resolve.

**Subheadline**

> Sua rotina já acontece no WhatsApp. Continue conversando com seus alunos normalmente — o Lob, seu agente de IA pessoal, ajuda a transformar mensagens, horários e pendências em uma operação organizada, sem mudar a maneira como já interage com seus clientes.

**CTA ideas**

- `Quero conhecer o Lob`
- `Entrar na lista de espera`
- `Quero testar`
- `Avise-me quando lançar`

For a pre-launch domain placeholder, **"Quero conhecer o Lob"** or **"Avise-me quando lançar"** avoids implying that the product is already generally available.

## 14. Tone of Voice

The brand should be:

- simple;
- conversational;
- intelligent without sounding technical;
- confident without sounding corporate;
- lightly playful;
- familiar to tennis players;
- focused on relief rather than automation hype.

Avoid excessive terms such as:

- AI-powered;
- revolutionary;
- intelligent automation platform;
- omnichannel;
- productivity ecosystem;
- digital transformation.

AI is an enabling technology, not necessarily the headline.

Prefer language about the user's actual life:

- "seus alunos";
- "sua agenda";
- "suas mensagens";
- "sua semana";
- "ganhar tempo";
- "deixar com o Lob";
- "voltar pro jogo".

## 15. Visual Direction

The tennis metaphor can appear subtly in the visual identity.

Possible elements:

- the curved trajectory of a lob as a recurring graphic element;
- a tennis ball trajectory connecting WhatsApp conversations to an organized schedule;
- court geometry used as a subtle grid system;
- generous whitespace to reinforce the idea of breathing room / regained time;
- small tennis references rather than literal stock photography of players.

A particularly useful visual metaphor is:

```text
MESSAGES                         ORGANIZED ROUTINE

💬 09:12  Pode ser terça 15h?          ┌─────────────┐
💬 09:14  Fechado!             ╭───►   │ TER  15:00  │
💬 11:32  Quinta vou viajar   ╱        │ João        │
💬 11:35  Remarcamos semana? ╱         └─────────────┘
                             ╱
                          LOB
```

The arc itself can become part of the brand system.

---

## 16. What the Landing Page Should NOT Do Yet

The v0 page does not need to:

- explain the full architecture;
- explain WhatsApp API integrations;
- expose AI implementation details;
- describe every future module;
- promise functionality that has not yet been validated;
- position Lob as a generic solution for every profession immediately;
- include complex pricing.

The objective is primarily to establish the domain, concept and identity and potentially capture early interest.

## 19. The Principle to Preserve

As the product evolves, preserve this idea:

> **The user should not work for the software. The software should work around the user's existing workflow.**

WhatsApp is not merely an integration channel.

It is where the user's operational reality already exists.

**Lob's job is to turn that reality into structure — and give the professional their time back.**
